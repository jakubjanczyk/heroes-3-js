# Trading and services

Roadmap 7.16, with the Marketplace of 6.2: trading resources in a town's Marketplace and at the Trading Post, then the map's other services. Nothing of it exists yet: the Marketplace can be built (`game/data/buildings.json`) and is drawn on the town picture, but clicking it does nothing.

## Parts

Too large for one PR. Split:

- **7.16.1 The Marketplace** (6.2): clicking it on the town picture opens the trading window; the player trades one resource for another at a rate set by how many Marketplaces they own.
- **7.16.2 The Trading Post**: a map object a hero visits to open the same window, at the rate of five Marketplaces.
- The rest of 7.16 stays as it is on the roadmap, one part each when it comes up: Black Market, University, Hill Fort, Refugee Camp, Sanctuary, Den of Thieves, Altar of Sacrifice, War Machine Factory, Shipyard.

## 7.16.1 The Marketplace

Checked against the Heroes 3 wiki's raw page source (Marketplace, its rate table `Template:Marketplace`, Trading Post, `Translation_Data/GENRLTXT.TXT`) and the reference engine's market rules and market window.

### Rules

- **Who trades.** The town's owner, from the town screen, with or without a hero in the town (the engine opens the window from the town; the wiki's Marketplace page asks for nothing else). A town without a Marketplace has nothing to click.
- **The rate depends on the Marketplaces the player owns**, in all their towns, one per town; nine or more give the best rate (wiki; the engine counts one per town with a Marketplace). The rate is the same in each of the player's towns.
- **The rates** (wiki's table), for 1 to 9+ Marketplaces. Basic resources are Wood and Ore, rare ones Mercury, Sulfur, Crystal and Gems:

  | # | Gold → basic | Gold → rare | Basic → gold | Basic → basic | Basic → rare | Rare → gold | Rare → basic | Rare → rare |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | 1 | 2500 → 1 | 5000 → 1 | 1 → 25 | 10 → 1 | 20 → 1 | 1 → 50 | 5 → 1 | 10 → 1 |
  | 2 | 1667 → 1 | 3333 → 1 | 1 → 37 | 7 → 1 | 13 → 1 | 1 → 75 | 3 → 1 | 7 → 1 |
  | 3 | 1250 → 1 | 2500 → 1 | 1 → 50 | 5 → 1 | 10 → 1 | 1 → 100 | 3 → 1 | 5 → 1 |
  | 4 | 1000 → 1 | 2000 → 1 | 1 → 62 | 4 → 1 | 8 → 1 | 1 → 125 | 2 → 1 | 4 → 1 |
  | 5 | 833 → 1 | 1667 → 1 | 1 → 75 | 3 → 1 | 7 → 1 | 1 → 150 | 2 → 1 | 3 → 1 |
  | 6 | 714 → 1 | 1429 → 1 | 1 → 88 | 3 → 1 | 6 → 1 | 1 → 175 | 1 → 1 | 3 → 1 |
  | 7 | 625 → 1 | 1250 → 1 | 1 → 100 | 3 → 1 | 5 → 1 | 1 → 200 | 1 → 1 | 3 → 1 |
  | 8 | 556 → 1 | 1111 → 1 | 1 → 112 | 2 → 1 | 4 → 1 | 1 → 225 | 1 → 1 | 2 → 1 |
  | 9+ | 500 → 1 | 1000 → 1 | 1 → 125 | 2 → 1 | 4 → 1 | 1 → 250 | 1 → 1 | 2 → 1 |

  The engine works them out: a resource is worth 1 (gold), 250 (Wood, Ore) or 500 (the rare ones); the market gives `(Marketplaces + 1) / 20` of the wanted resource's worth, at most a half. When the given resource is worth more, one of it buys `ceil(given / wanted)`; otherwise `round(wanted / given)` of it buy one. That matches every cell of the wiki's table but three: one Wood or Ore sells for 38, 63 and 113 gold at 2, 4 and 8 Marketplaces, where the wiki says 37, 62 and 112. We keep the wiki's table as data (agreed with the user).
- **A trade** is a whole number of the rate's lots: picking 1 → 25 and four lots gives 4 Wood for 100 gold; with 10 → 1, four lots give 40 Wood for 4 Ore. The player can trade at most what they have (the engine's trade and its slider).
- **Trading a resource for itself** isn't offered: its rate reads "n/a" (`core.genrltxt.164`) and Trade stays off (engine).

### The window

The engine's layout, from the market window code, and a screenshot of the game's window the user shared (Gems picked on the left, Gold on the right, five Marketplaces):

- Title **"Marketplace"** (`core.genrltxt.158`), on a red frame over the town screen.
- Two halves. Left, **"Kingdom Resources"** (`core.genrltxt.270`): the seven resources in rows of three, three and one (Wood, Mercury, Ore; Sulfur, Crystal, Gems; Gold), each with the amount the player has under it. Right, under the merchant's box, **"Available for Trade"** (`core.genrltxt.168`): the same seven in the same rows. A picked resource has a yellow frame. Once one on the left is picked, each on the right shows its rate: what one lot gives, and `/` what one lot costs when that is more than one (`25` for Wood to gold; `1/10` for Wood to Ore; `n/a` for Wood itself).
- The merchant's line at the top:
  - nothing picked yet: "Please inspect our fine wares.  If you feel like offering a trade, click on the items you wish to trade with and for." (`core.genrltxt.163`);
  - both picked: "I can offer you %d %s of %s for %d %s of %s." (`core.genrltxt.157`), with one lot's numbers, "unit" or "units" (`core.genrltxt.161`, `160`) and the resource names: "I can offer you 25 units of Gold for 1 unit of Wood.";
  - after a trade: "You have received quite a bargain.  I expect to make no profit on the deal.  Can I interest you in any of my other wares?" (`core.genrltxt.162`). A trade clears both picks.
- The merchant's line sits in a box at the top of the right half.
- A red strip under the panels: the picked resource to give with the amount being given (0 at first), "QTY TO TRADE" with the slider for the number of lots under it (from 0 to as many as the player can pay), and the picked resource to receive with the amount received. Its wording "QTY TO TRADE" is on the game's picture, not in its texts.
- Buttons along the bottom: Max (arrows), **Trade** (a handshake), and **Done** (a tick) on the right. Trade is off with nothing to trade (no pick, the same resource, 0 lots). The button at the bottom left opens giving resources to another player, left for later: it isn't drawn until then.
- **Done** (Enter or Escape) closes it, back to the town screen.

### What we decided

- The window is ours in look: the same panels and texts, drawn with the game's frames as the other town windows are. On a phone the two panels stack, as the Tavern's do.
- A trade is saved as one fact, `fact.resources.traded`, with what was given and what was received, so a reload keeps it (one fact per decision, 9.5). Opening the window isn't saved, as the Hall isn't.
- The resource bar updates at once.
- The rates are data: `game/data/marketplace.json` gives each resource its kind (gold, basic, rare) and, for each pair of kinds, the rate at one to nine Marketplaces. `game/heroes/marketplace.js` reads a rate from it and works out a trade's amounts.
- The computer doesn't trade yet (8.4, "saving up").

### Left for later

- **The Artifact Merchants, the Freelancer's Guild** and the Bulwark's Fair: with their factions (6.5).
- **Status bar and right-click help** inside the window (the engine's `zelp` texts): no town window has them yet.
- **The Resource Silo**'s income, the other half of the 6.2 line, stays with 6.2.
