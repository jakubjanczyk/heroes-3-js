# Combat AI (2026-10-20)

Roadmap 5.7b, kept simple: the monster's stacks take their turns, and a battle with no hero stack left is lost. Follows `docs/notes/2026-10-20-monster-stacks-double-wide-and-battle-cursors.md`.

## How Heroes 3 does it

The game's combat AI weighs the damage each stack can deal and take, shoots with shooters, waits and defends. We start much simpler and leave the rest for later (roadmap 5.7b, "Smarter combat AI").

When the hero's army dies, the hero is lost: it leaves the map, and the monster stays with the creatures it has left.

## What we do

- **Picking a target.** A monster stack goes for the nearest living hero stack: the fewest hexes to a hex next to it from which it can attack, counted around other stacks for walkers and in a straight line for flyers. On a tie, the hero stack in the earlier army slot, then the first such hex in the order of `getHexesNextTo`.
- **Attacking or closing in.** If that hex is within the stack's speed it attacks from there, with the same damage, retaliation and log as the player's attacks. Otherwise a walker walks as far as its speed takes it along the way, and a flyer lands on the hex in reach nearest that hex. A stack that cannot get any closer lets its turn pass.
- **Shooters** walk to the nearest enemy too until there are ranged attacks (5.6).
- The rules are in `game/heroes/battle-ai.js` (`planComputerTurn`), with `getApproaches` in `game/heroes/battle.js` listing every hex next to an enemy a stack can get to and how far it is.
- `battle.module.js` plays the monster's turn whenever its stack is active: at the start, after each move or attack, and after a reload. It saves the same `fact.battle.stack.moved` / `fact.battle.stack.attacked` facts as the player's actions, with the damage rolled, so a reload replays them; a stack that does nothing saves `fact.battle.turn.ended`. The walk is shown as the player's is. Clicks on the battlefield do nothing until a hero stack is active again.
- **Defeat.** When no hero stack is left, the battle ends (`fact.battle.ended` with `winner: 'defender'`), the monster's count becomes what it has left (`fact.monster.count.changed`, read by the status bar and the right-click popup) and the hero is removed from the map (`fact.entity.removed` with the hero's id). The hero no longer moves.
- Auto combat still wins the battle outright.
