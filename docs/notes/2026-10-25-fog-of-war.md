# Fog of war (2026-10-25)

Roadmap 7.1, and step 5 of `docs/notes/2026-10-25-game-state-store.md`.

## The rules

- A tile is unexplored (black) or explored. An explored tile stays revealed and shows what is on it now; nothing is dimmed or shown as last seen.
- A tile is in sight when its distance from the one who sees, rounded to a whole number, is at most the sight radius: with radius 5, (5, 2) at 5.39 is in sight, (5, 3) at 5.83 and (4, 4) at 5.66 are not.
- A hero sees 5 tiles, after every step it takes. An owned town sees 5 tiles around its entrance, at the start and when it is captured. Any other object the player owns at the start, such as a mine, sees 3. Capturing a mine reveals nothing more.
- Unexplored tiles are blocked for routes. A click on one does nothing, the cursor over one is the arrow, the status bar is empty, and right-clicking one shows "Uncharted Territory".
- The minimap draws unexplored tiles black, with no town marker or mine colour on them.

## What we do

- `game/state/fog.js` keeps each player's explored tiles in the `fog` slice and holds the sight rule; `game/heroes/state/fog.js` says who sees how far and which facts reveal tiles (`fact.hero.moved`, `fact.town.captured`). Fog is rebuilt on replay, so a rule change applies to old sessions too.
- `fog-view` draws a black tile over each unexplored one in `.fog-layer`, between the objects and the path preview, and removes the covers as tiles are explored.
- A scenario has fog of war unless it sets `fogOfWar: false`. Behavior tests turn it off unless they are about fog.

## Not yet

- Soft edges where unexplored tiles meet explored ones (with terrain sprites, 10.2).
- Scouting (4.7) and buildings that see further, such as the Lookout Tower (6.5).
- Other players' fog (8.1): views already read the viewing player's.
