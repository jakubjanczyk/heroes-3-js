# Tavern (2026-10-25)

Roadmap 4.5.4: hiring heroes in a town's Tavern.

## How Heroes 3 does it

- **The window.** Opened by clicking the Tavern on the town picture. Across the top the tavern's picture and the barkeep's rumour ("After a generous tip, the barkeep whispers: …"). Under them "Heroes for Hire": two portraits, none picked when the window opens, then the one clicked framed with a line about them ("Tazar is a level 1 Beastmaster with 0 artifacts."). Beside them the cost, 2500 gold, and the Recruit button. Along the bottom the Thieves' Guild button, Close and the window's status bar ("Select Tazar" over a portrait).
- **Right click** on a portrait shows the hero's box, as on the map: portrait, name, primary skills and army.
- **The heroes.** Both taverns of a player offer the same two heroes. At the start of each week both are replaced by new ones. A hero who is on the map is never offered. When a hero is hired, a new one takes their place.
- **Starting army.** A hired hero comes with up to three stacks of their faction's first three creatures. One roll from 1 to 100 decides how many: the first stack always, the second on 88 or less, the third on 25 or less. Each stack's size is rolled between its minimum and maximum.
- **Hiring.** The hero comes out at the town's entrance as its visiting hero, with full movement points, and becomes the selected hero. Recruit can't be used before a hero is picked, without the gold, with 8 heroes already, or while a hero stands in the town.
- **Rumours.** A new rumour each week: one of the game's random rumours, the map's own, or one about the other players (the strongest army, the highest income) or the Grail.

## What we do

- `game/data/tavern.json` holds the cost, the pool of heroes for hire (a few Knights) and the rumours. The rumours are our own; the game's come from its data files. The Knight's starting army is `startingArmy` in `hero-classes.json`: Pikemen 10-20, Archers 4-7, Griffins 2-3 (no war machines yet).
- `game/heroes/tavern.js` holds the rules: rolling the heroes for hire and their armies, the rumour, the hero's line and whether a hero can be hired.
- The `tavern` slice (`game/heroes/state/tavern.js`) keeps each player's offer: `{ heroes, rumour }`. It is rolled the first time a tavern opens in a week and saved as `fact.tavern.heroesOffered`, so a reload shows the same heroes. A new week (`fact.turn.ended` on day 1) clears it.
- Hiring (`command.tavern.hire.requested`) is one commit: `fact.resources.spent`, `fact.hero.hired` (the new hero's id, owner, tile, name, class and army), the offer with the hired hero replaced, and `fact.hero.selected`. The core `heroes` slice and the `heroDetails` slice add the hero, so the hero list, the minimap, the map, routes and meeting all pick them up. The hero's id is `hired-hero-<n>`.
- Heroes are drawn where the state has them (`heroEntity` in `game/state/queries.js`), so a hero who wasn't in the scenario is drawn too.
- `app/heroes/modules/tavern.module.js` decides; `tavern-view.module.js` draws the window from `#tavern-window-template` and the hero box popup (`renderHeroBox`). Escape closes the window.
