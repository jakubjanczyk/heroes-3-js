# Cursors (2026-10-19)

Roadmap 1.2. The adventure map uses Heroes 3's cursors rather than the roadmap's original idea of boots and a red X.

## Behaviour

- The whole game shows the H3 arrow instead of the browser cursor. Buttons no longer switch to a hand, as in H3.
- Over the map, the cursor says what clicking the tile under the mouse would do:

| Under the mouse | Cursor |
|---|---|
| The hero | Hero helmet (`hero`) |
| Empty tile with a route | Horse (`move`) |
| Monster | Crossed swords (`attack`) |
| Resource, treasure chest, mine entrance, town entrance | Rearing horse (`visit`) |
| Water, rock, a mine's or town's building, a tile with no route | Arrow (`arrow`) |

- The horse, swords and rearing horse show no number when the hero gets there today. Otherwise they show the day it arrives, 2 to 4. Anything further away also shows 4, as in H3.
- The day count follows the movement rules: today's points left, then a full day's points for each day after.
- The cursor updates when the hero moves, a monster or resource is removed, or movement points change, without the mouse moving. With the mouse over the tile the hero walks to, the cursor turns into the helmet when the hero arrives.

## How it works

- `app/heroes/modules/adventure-cursor.module.js` listens to `ui.tile.hovered`, asks the movement module for the route (`command.route.requested` / `fact.route.planned`, the same as clicks) and sets `data-cursor` on the viewport.
- `game/domain/movement/travel-days.js` counts the day the hero reaches the end of a route. `game/heroes/adventure-cursor.js` picks the cursor.
- `game/ui/cursors.css` maps each `data-cursor` to a picture in `assets/cursors`. The arrow's hotspot is its tip. The others are centred on the tile under the mouse.
- The pictures are pixel art on a 16x16 grid, saved at 32x32 by `node scripts/build-cursors.mjs`. Edit the rows there and re-run it.
- Behavior tests read the cursor with `getMapCursor()` / `getCursorOver(element)`, from the computed `cursor` of the element under the mouse.
